# "Hacker-Light" Style - Bright dev desk, pixel-art, big icons

Light-mode version of the hacker world. Warm cream background with a softly blurred BRIGHT developer desk behind (curved monitors with faint code, daylight room, airy). 8-bit pixel-art mascot in a bright orange hoodie. Bold condensed sans headlines mixing near-black and bright orange #E95223. Big flat app icons as slide heroes. Handwritten annotations + hand-drawn curved arrows. White cards with thin orange borders and monospace text.

Mood: techie but friendly, premium, playful, save-worthy. Proven on Albert's winning tech-stack carousel.

Keep prompts in English. Accent is always #E95223 unless overridden.

## Visual DNA (put this block at the top of EVERY content-slide prompt)

```
Match the reference cover EXACTLY: same warm cream off-white background,
softly blurred bright developer desk far behind, same bold condensed sans
typography mixing near-black and vivid bright orange #E95223, same
handwritten footer (Instagram glyph + '@albert.olgaard' bottom-left,
bookmark glyph + 'save for later' bottom-right), same 8-bit pixel-art
mascot style with bright orange hoodie. LIGHT mode, no dark background.
```

Core elements:

- **Background:** warm cream off-white (#F4F0E8), blurred bright dev desk (two curved monitors, faint code, daylight), subtle paper grain. Never dark, never sky/meadow.
- **Accent:** vivid bright orange #E95223. Always add "bold, saturated" and "not muted, not brownish terracotta" in the cover prompt or the model drifts warm-brown.
- **Mascot:** flat 2D pixel-art (8-bit, hard pixels, NOT 3D): friendly pixel guy, square head, bright orange hoodie, grey shorts. Optional tiny bright orange pixel critter sidekick. One on-theme gag per slide.
- **Headlines:** HUGE bold condensed sans (Anton style), near-black words alternating with bright orange words, hand-drawn orange underline under the key word.
- **Cards:** white rounded cards, thin bright orange border, soft shadow, monospace text, exactly 3 short lines.
- **Annotations:** handwritten dark-ink marker line + hand-drawn curved arrow pointing at the hero element.
- **Footer:** IG glyph + '@albert.olgaard' bottom-left handwritten; bookmark + 'save for later' bottom-right handwritten italic.
- End every prompt with: `No carousel dots, no colored emojis, no screenshots.` (add `no photos of people` on cover and CTA).

## Comparison slide template

Two white rounded panels side by side. LEFT header muted grey ('CHATTING' / old way) + 3 grey mono lines. RIGHT header bright orange (the new way) + 3 near-black mono lines. Mascot points at the right panel.

## Recap "stack grid" slide template

Big bright orange starburst (Claude) or the deck's hero icon on the left, hand-drawn black curved arrow into a big white rounded card with thin black border, handwritten label 'Tech Stack:' or 'connected to:' above, and a neat grid of ALL the deck's app icons inside (each described briefly from the library). Mascot plugs a pixel cable into the card or stands proud with arms crossed.

holds a credit card (payments), waters the leaf (database), climbs an arrow like a ladder (CRM), launches a rocket (deploy), magnifying glass (search/vectors), talks into a phone with sound waves (voice), checks off a to-do list (tasks), writes with a big pencil (docs), points at a rising bar chart (tracking), sends a paper plane (newsletter), fires envelopes from a cannon (cold email), shrugs between two icons (comparison), plugs in a cable (connect), sips coffee with sun rays (morning), pixel shield + fleeing bug (security), teaches a pixel robot at a whiteboard (skills), wears a headset with a scoreboard (calls), relaxes in a deck chair while envelopes fly (automation), waves (CTA).

## Hacker-light-specific rules

- Light background ALWAYS. If the model returns anything dark, regenerate.
- Icons instead of screenshots, always. The icon is the hero of the slide.
- 3 monospace benefit lines per card, no more.
- 5th-grade reading level on all viewer copy. Short sentences. No em dashes.
- One mascot gag per slide, matched to the slide topic.
