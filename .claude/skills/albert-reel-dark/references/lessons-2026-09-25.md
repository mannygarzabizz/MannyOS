## Process lessons

1. **A stamp comes with sound.** This one had two `mix.py` cues, `thud.wav` at −17 dB ("GONE stamp") and `whoosh-snap`
   at −28 dB ("stamp whoosh"). When you remove an overlay, remove its cues too, or the thud plays over nothing.
2. **Weak payoff → push the physical action, not a word.** Make the drain faster, land the counter with its frame, or
   make the card empty or shake. Never lay a word on top.
3. **Test any on-screen word:** would it be on the real screen being recreated? A real button, a status chip or the
   product's own copy is fine. A word that only repeats the spoken line is a label. Cut it.
4. **Without the chip the CTA still has to pass `check_motion`.** The keyword types, the comment posts, and it gets a
   like on the last word, as in the 22/23 Sep CTAs.
