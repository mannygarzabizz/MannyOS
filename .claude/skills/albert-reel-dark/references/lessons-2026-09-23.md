## Process lessons (each one cost time)

1. **The OBS capture is not always the prompter.** This time it was a static Claude Code window; the script is the
   Scribe transcript. Grab four frames at 5/50/75/95 % before assuming a prompter frame exists.
2. **The brief can reference a line he never recorded** ("just saved your usage limit"). Search the transcript and the
   untranscribed-speech scan before building for it; put the asset on the nearest real line and flag it first.
3. **Scribe mishears the DM keyword** ("just comment SAVE" → "just come and save"). Look at the CTA tokens before
   choosing the keyword; merge in `fix_words.py`.
4. **Never `pkill` a running render without killing its ffmpeg encoders.** Python dies, the encoder keeps writing
   `clean-voice.mp4`, the restarted render writes the same path, and the FINAL comes out with "Invalid NAL unit size".
   Kill `render.py`, then `pkill -f clean-voice.mp4`, delete both renders, restart.
5. **`check_motion` does not see typing, slow cursors or 6 px floats.** What passes: a tile bounce/hop, a chip or banner
   appearing, a hover highlight moving, a light sweep across a card, a wash, a scroll. Add one real event per 0.5 s.
6. **`qa.py` had hard-coded beat indices** (B[15]) from the 17-beat reel; now generic in the template.
7. **A tile dropping in from above crosses the Instagram top band.** Declare the drop as an `fx` window rather than
   lowering the drop (a drop from inside the frame does not read as a drop).
8. **Hook photo + prop layout:** a held prop crosses the chest **below the chin**, never the face; arms (sleeve colour from
   the photo) reach out of the box to both grips; boxes half-in on frame 0; a rocket needs ≥ 170 px of flight to read
   (380 px boxes at x 20 and 680).
9. **Firecrawl can be out of credits (HTTP 402).** Fall back to WebSearch/WebFetch for the fact-check; the App Store
   icon route (iTunes Search API) and Wikimedia's API need no key.
10. **He revises a drawn gesture in small steps** ("smaller", "shorter arm", "hand down", "line thinner"). Preview every
    gesture as a 2× close-up crop (not the 270 px strip) before rendering, and keep the earlier FINAL as `-vN.mp4` each round.
11. **`at()` matches case-insensitively:** `at('write2','Use')` returned the earlier "use Fable"; pass `nth=1` for the second
    occurrence and check the transcript tokens for duplicates before wiring a beat.
12. **Thick polylines show seams at joints.** Draw arms and tubes as overlapping discs along the bezier (outline radius first,
    fill radius second); it also gives the 1.5 px outline that matches the hand.
13. **He interrupts renders with new notes.** Kill `render.py` AND its ffmpeg encoders, delete both renders, then restart -
    or the next FINAL is corrupt (lesson 4).
