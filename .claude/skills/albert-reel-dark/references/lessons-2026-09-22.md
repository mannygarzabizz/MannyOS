## Process lessons (each one bit me)

1. **Scribe word edges lag the sound.** A 0.5 s "pause" per Scribe was voiced on the 10 ms RMS profile. `build_cut_continuations.py`
   prints quiet runs ≥ 0.25 s inside every selected range — only tighten a gap the RMS confirms. Split a beat *without* a cut
   at a measured word onset (`fix_words_split.py` SPLIT) when the take is continuous.
2. **`beats-raw.json` goes stale.** `fix_words` splits beats from a raw copy; after any re-cut delete `edit/beats-raw.json` (or
   rebuild it from `edl.json`) or the split fails with `StopIteration` and beats.json keeps the old durations.
3. **Scribe spells numbers out** on some passes ("seven hundred and fifty", "four point six"). Merge the tokens in `fix_words`
   (`merge(['seven','hundred','and','fifty'],'750')`) so captions and `at()` see `750` / `4.6`.
4. **`at()` keeps trailing periods.** `bare()` strips punctuation except `.`, so `'this.'` ≠ `'this,'`. Look at the final
   transcript token before writing an `at()` lookup; for repeated words use `nth`.
5. **Smoke-test every beat before a full render** (start / middle / last frame of each beat through `render.frame`). Three
   renders died at frame 450+ on a NameError in a scene that the preview sheet never reached.
6. **Background wait: grep `^mixed|Traceback`, not `Error`.** ffmpeg prints "Error writing trailer: Broken pipe" *after* a
   Python crash; matching it made the gates run on the previous render and report a stale pass.
7. **One-frame lag at every cut** (fixed in `render.py`): beat starts carry float residue, compare whole frames.
   `check_frames.py` shows it as ink under the captions on exactly one frame per full beat.
8. **`check_motion.py` does not see small things.** Dots, ticks, a caret, a 40 px icon do not register at 270 px. What
   passes: a row fading to grey, a dropdown appearing, a chip floating ±6 px, a figure bobbing, axis ticks + a light sweep.
9. **Full-screen beats need something already landed on frame 0** (the first tile's onset at −0.10) or `check_frames` flags a
   blank first frame.
10. **Provenance with a lag search.** Plain correlation at the nominal offset reads 0.1–0.4 because of sub-millisecond concat
    rounding; `check_provenance.py` searches ±30 ms and every take reads 1.0000 at ≤ 0.5 ms.
11. **Replacing a scene section can delete a helper that lived inside it** (`_money` vanished twice). Keep shared helpers above
    the first scene, not between two scenes.
12. **Dense strips before shipping choreography.** `strips.py <beat> t0 t1 12` across every phase change; a snap is visible as
    a jump between adjacent frames long before Albert sees it.
13. **Continuity across a cut = one function, offset time.** When two beats share a table/prop, draw both from
    `state(t + previous_duration)` and skip `enter0` on the second beat.
14. **Reveal timing.** Albert wants the payoff visible *before* the payoff word: fire the reveal on the word before it and let
    the payoff word add a pulse. Three throws in 0.5 s "feels rushed"; ≈ 0.9 s apart across beats is right.
15. **When he says "premium"**: gradients on props, a real table board with a soft shadow, contact shadows under objects, a
    shadow under a hat brim, a glow behind the payoff, curved limbs with hands. Flat silhouettes read as "ass".
16. **Icons from the internet, never drawn figures.** Sources: Apple Color Emoji (single codepoints, native 160 px,
    downscale only); `raw.githubusercontent.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji/main/assets/<Name>/3D/<name>_3d.png` (people under
    `<Name>/Default/3D/<name>_3d_default.png`); `itunes.apple.com/search?term=<app>&entity=software` → `artworkUrl512`.
    Countries from GeoJSON. The Claude tile (+🎩, terracotta arms) is the only drawn character.
17. **Fact-check outcome patterns.** A sourced number → recreate the source page (`article_zoom.py`, Foundation Capital).
    An unsourced spoken number → keep it on screen (it is spoken), flag it first, offer the sourced line for a re-record.
    A dropped word in the last take ("forever") → last take still wins, flag it with the one-line fix.
18. **Report structure that worked:** flag first, what he asked in his words → what shipped, sources, cut table, scene table,
    sound, verification (signal-only, say so), not done.
