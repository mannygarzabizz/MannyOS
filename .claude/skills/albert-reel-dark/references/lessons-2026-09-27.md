# Lessons, 27 Sep 2026: first dark-mode reel ("Did AI just kill Coinbase?")

The skill became albert-reel-dark. The first reel built with it: `content/2026-09-27-ai-kill-coinbase/`.

1. **Gates must read the ground from `draw.py`.** `check_frames.py` hardcoded the old off-white `#F1F0EB`. On the dark
   ground every pixel read as "ink", so it reported y 0..1916 and "something under the words" on every beat. It now
   imports `draw.BG`. Any new gate or helper that compares against the ground must do the same.
2. **Limiter ceiling for AAC.** With `alimiter=limit=.84` the mixed WAV peaked at −2.3 dBTP. AAC then lifted a caption
   click in the first 0.25 s to −0.3 dBTP. The template limiter is now `.68`, which gave −1.8 dBTP and −17.0 LUFS on
   this reel. Always measure `ebur128=peak=true` on the final MP4, not on the WAV.
3. **Translucent edges.** `rr()` now blends translucent outlines as well as fills. The old path wrote
   `(255,255,255,26)` straight into the alpha channel and punched see-through rings into dark cards.
4. **Fish's 40 ms grid and cut boundaries.** Fish placed a beat's first word 10–30 ms *before* its cut ("set" of
   `fees`, "And" of `ourselves`), so captions and `at()` found it in the previous beat. `fix_words.py` now snaps any
   word within 40 ms before a beat start onto that beat.
5. **Motion gate on subtle UI.** A progress arc filling and a finished card read as "still" at 270 px. The accepted
   fixes: a thicker arc with a bright head, the icon breathing while it waits, `sweep()` (a blurred light band) after
   a landing, and a slow 5 % push-in on the last beat.
6. **Dark-on-dark props.** The `#111113` gun vanished on `#0A0A0B`, so it is gunmetal `#3A3A40` / `#66666E` now.
   Split-flap tiles are `CARD2` with a hairline and a black seam. The "?" tile is `#2C2C2E` with a light ring.
7. **Products in their own dark theme** read best and need no translation: X dark for the post, Whop's own dark
   dashboard (it is dark by default), Instagram's dark comment sheet, dark Safari chrome.
